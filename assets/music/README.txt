DROP BACKGROUND MUSIC HERE
==========================

Put one or more royalty-free .mp3 files in this folder and the renderer will
pick one at random per episode and mix it under the narration at low volume
(MUSIC_VOLUME in .env, default 0.12).

Leave this folder empty and videos render with narration only. That works fine.

Free, genuinely license-clear sources:
  - YouTube Audio Library  (studio.youtube.com -> Audio Library)
  - Pixabay Music          (pixabay.com/music)
  - Free Music Archive     (freemusicarchive.org - check each track's licence)

For this kind of emotional story content, look for tags like "cinematic",
"emotional piano", "sad", "inspirational", "dramatic".

Short tracks are fine - anything shorter than the narration is looped
automatically.
